About

I'm a Data & Cloud Security Engineer at The Home Depot, with cloud experience across AWS, Azure and GCP. I'm AWS Certified (Solutions Architect – Associate), spent nearly two years working in Azure environments at Veracode and the Pennsylvania Turnpike Commission, and currently work in GCP day to day.

At The Home Depot my work is GCP (GKE) clusters — tightening Kubernetes configs and fine-tuning Akamai WAF/CDN policies against the OWASP Top 10 so production traffic stays fast and secure, with Terraform for automation.
